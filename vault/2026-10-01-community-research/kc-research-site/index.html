Sales, total competition and campaign fit come first.

01Before committing

Verify the campaign.

Check that the exact product and your planned content qualify while participation is still available.

Open campaign checklist +
  • Match the exact ASIN, variant and eligible seller.
  • Confirm at least 5% applicable commission. Record whether it is a bonus, base or combined rate.
  • Check dates, remaining budget and open participation. Exclude full or exhausted campaigns.
  • Read the content requirements and confirm onsite eligibility for your planned video.
  • Allow time for delivery, filming and the required content submission.

A listed campaign or a percentage alone does not establish eligibility.

02Before choosing

Check real demand.

Use fresh product metrics and the total influencer count. Keep the score as supporting context.

Open product checklist +
  • Confirm estimated 30-day product revenue of at least $10,000. Record units sold separately when available.
  • Verify no more than 5 total influencer videos, including videos not currently displayed.
  • Confirm an existing video section. Distinguish a top merchant video from bottom-only placement.
  • Wait for fresh exact-ASIN data. Investigate conflicting counts instead of selecting the lower number.
  • Prioritize home, cleaning, kitchen, toys and family crafts. Children’s clothing is eligible; men’s products and adult apparel are excluded for this round.

Product sales are not your income. Zero influencer videos does not guarantee placement.

03Before spending

Confirm your cost.

Check what you already own, the live purchase price and whether a sample was actually offered.

Open purchase checklist +
  • Identify products already bought and brands already contacted to avoid repeats.
  • For self-funded purchases, confirm a live price of $100 or less, stock and the exact variant.
  • Treat “Samples: No,” a mention of limited samples and a confirmed sample offer as different facts.
  • If using the calculator, compare Standard and Top Carousel assumptions and include delivery and filming time.
  • Keep projected commissions and payback dates clearly labeled as estimates.

These are review steps, not approval to buy or accept a campaign.

Viral Score ↗

Viral Vue describes the score as a composite indicator and explicitly cautions that low scores do not always rule out an opportunity and high scores do not guarantee success. The page does not independently establish the October 1 equation-revision timeline.

30-Day Sales – Amazon ↗

Units sold and revenue are different fields. Viral Vue describes revenue as the past 30 days of sales multiplied by current product price. It is product-level demand information, not the creator’s commission.

Total Influencer Videos ↗

The total includes videos linked to the product that are not currently displayed in either carousel. Opening the count reveals the linked videos. A visible top-carousel count is therefore not a substitute for total competition.

Top Carousel ↗

Merchant videos enable the top carousel, but influencer placement depends on Amazon selection, availability and brand permissions. Placement does not guarantee sales.

Break Even Calculator ↗

Top Carousel mode assumes top placement. Standard mode factors in total influencer-video competition. Inputs include units sold, price, commission, CC bonus, campaign days and content lead time. The documented default attribution assumption is one sale per 100 units, adjustable to account performance. These are scenario assumptions, not KC’s measured conversion rate or a guaranteed payback date. The page’s FAQ explanation of attribution appears mismatched with its input instructions; the explicit input description is used here.

Amazon onsite earnings ↗

Amazon may select eligible storefront content for onsite display. Onsite earnings depend on qualifying customer activity; simply uploading content does not establish placement or earnings.

Creator Connections Associates Policy ↗

Read in authorized Chrome; displayed update February 2, 2026. Section 1.6 contemplates program samples and requires genuine, independent content based on actual experience. Section 2.1 ties compensation to the particular request, specified products, dates and evidence containing a related Special Link. Its wording should not be replaced by a blanket forum assurance that any onsite video qualifies. Section 3 separately describes Sponsored Products for Creators and CPC earnings; do not confuse that mechanism with an Affiliate+ percentage bonus.

Unresolved documentation detail
The Amazon getting-started PDF exceeded the retrieval limit. Its indexed excerpt mentioned onsite/offsite reporting and budget exhaustion, but was not used as blanket proof of onsite-only eligibility. The current policy was read in Chrome; campaign-specific eligibility remains to be checked.

No messages, reactions, campaign acceptances or purchases were performed. The product shortlist and Google Doc were not edited.
