Explicit restrictions

Two limits that shape production.

Amazon-provided images are restricted.

The affiliate license generally prohibits altering Amazon-provided “Program Content,” with narrow exceptions such as proportional image resizing. AI animation, recoloring, and background replacement fall outside those stated exceptions.

Use KC’s own photos or assets separately licensed for the planned edits. Amazon also restricts using Program Content to develop or improve AI models; this is different from a general ban on AI-assisted production.

Read the IP license and usage requirements ↗

Make a separate storefront edit.

Amazon’s shoppable-video guidelines prohibit prices, discount claims, and other time-sensitive promotional information. They also restrict impersonation, infringement, incorrect product tagging, and promotion that directs viewers away from Amazon.

Tag only products clearly featured. Brand gifts or paid relationships need clear disclosure in the video.

Disclosure

Affiliate disclosure and AI disclosure are separate.

Disclose the commission relationship.

Amazon requires this statement on the site or associated with the social account:

“As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.”

Also disclose near affiliate recommendations and links. For social videos, include the commission disclosure in the video and near the links; a profile disclosure alone is insufficient.

Suggested plain-language wording: “I earn a commission if you purchase through my links.” Disclose brand payments and gifts separately when applicable.

Amazon disclosure requirements [5] · FTC placement guidance [6]

Upload requirement still to verify

Flag synthetic people where required.

Logie reports an Influencer storefront upload checkbox for AI-generated people. KC’s upload screen was not independently checked, so this remains a reported workflow requirement, not a verified account observation. [7]

Amazon separately confirms synthetic-person metadata requirements for seller listings and A+ content. That seller policy does not establish approval for influencer uploads. [8]

Production recommendation: clearly identify realistic simulations and synthetic presenters, and follow each publishing platform’s applicable requirements. A disclosure cannot fix false product claims or fabricated experiences. [4]

Recommended workflow for KC

Film once. Let AI help with the finish.

An animated introduction followed by KC’s real vacuum demonstration is a stronger starting point than generated footage of a stain removal she never tested.

  1. Capture the real item.Record actual use and KC’s honest observations.
  2. Add creative support.Draft captions, clean audio, and add illustrative graphics using assets we have rights to edit.
  3. Review the result.Check appearance, product facts, implied claims, and any synthetic scenes.
  4. Prepare each version.Add disclosures, make the Amazon-specific edit, and check current upload requirements.
